Setting up

  1. In Meta Business Manager, create a WhatsApp Business account and register the university's number (WhatsApp Cloud API).
  2. Create a System User access token with whatsapp_business_messaging permission. Put it in the server's .env as WHATSAPP_TOKEN=…, the app secret as WHATSAPP_APP_SECRET=… and a webhook password of your choice as WHATSAPP_VERIFY_TOKEN=…, then restart the web service.
  3. Enter the phone number ID and account ID above.
  4. Create the message templates in WhatsApp Manager (category Utility), wait for approval, then enter their names here and mark them active.
  5. In the Meta app's WhatsApp webhook settings use the callback URL {{ webhook_url }} with your verify token, and subscribe to messages (delivery statuses).
  6. Test the connection and send a test message, then switch on automatic reminders.

Reminders are queued at the dispatch time and sent by the whatsapp_tick job (every 5 minutes; the installer schedules it). Faculty must opt in first (their WhatsApp page, or the exam office records a signed consent).
